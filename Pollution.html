01 / THE PROBLEM

Pollution comes in many forms.

Pollution happens when harmful substances or energy enter our environment and disturb the natural balance of ecosystems.

01
🌫️

Air Pollution

Harmful gases and tiny particles released by vehicles, industries, burning fuels and other sources can make the air unsafe to breathe.

02
💧

Water Pollution

Chemicals, sewage, plastic and other waste can contaminate rivers, lakes, oceans and groundwater.

03
🗑️

Land Pollution

Excess waste, plastics and hazardous substances can damage soil, ecosystems and the organisms that depend on healthy land.

04
🔊

Noise Pollution

Excessive or unwanted sound from traffic, construction and machinery can affect people and wildlife.

02 / THE IMPACT

Nothing lives in isolation.

Environmental pollution can travel through ecosystems, affecting people, animals, plants and entire communities.

🫁 Human Health

Air pollution can contribute to respiratory and cardiovascular disease. Contaminated water and hazardous chemicals can create additional health risks.

🐦 Wildlife

Plastic, contaminated habitats, noise and polluted water can interfere with feeding, breeding, migration and survival.

🌱 Ecosystems

Pollution can disrupt soil, freshwater and marine ecosystems, reducing biodiversity and changing ecological relationships.

🌊 Oceans

Plastic waste and other pollutants can enter waterways and eventually reach the ocean, where they can harm marine organisms.

03 / THE RESPONSE

Small actions scale.

No single person can solve pollution. But millions of everyday choices can change demand, habits and systems.

♻️

Reduce & Reuse

Buy only what you need and reuse products whenever possible before sending them to the waste stream.

🚲

Cleaner Transport

Walking, cycling, public transport and shared journeys can reduce fuel use and emissions.

💡

Save Energy

Turn off unused appliances and choose efficient technology where practical.

💧

Protect Water

Avoid wasting water and keep chemicals, oils and waste away from drains and waterways.

🌳

Protect Nature

Trees, wetlands, forests and other ecosystems provide habitats and valuable environmental services.

🛍️

Choose Wisely

Consider durability, packaging, repairability and environmental impact when buying products.
